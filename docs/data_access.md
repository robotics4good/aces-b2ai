# Data Access Instructions

## Step 1 — PhysioNet Account
Create or log in at https://physionet.org

## Step 2 — Credentialing
Complete credentialing on PhysioNet (takes 1–3 days)

## Step 3 — Sign the DUA
Sign the Bridge2AI Voice Registered Access Agreement on the dataset page:
https://physionet.org/content/b2ai-voice-pediatric/1.0.0/

## Step 4 — Download
Download dataset files into the local `data/` folder in this repo.
This folder is gitignored — never commit data to GitHub.

## Step 5 — Raw Audio (optional)
Raw WAV files require separate controlled-access request:
Contact DACO@b2ai-voice.org

## DUA Compliance Reminders
- Do not share participant-level data outside the team
- Do not attempt to re-identify participants
- Cite the dataset in all outputs
